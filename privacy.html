Privacy

Half Marathon Coach is a personal training app used only by its owner.

It reads recovery, sleep, cycle, workout and profile data from WHOOP to adjust a training plan. That data is fetched when the page loads, shown in the browser, and not stored on any server or shared with anyone.

WHOOP sign-in tokens are kept in an encrypted cookie in your own browser. Disconnecting WHOOP in the app deletes that cookie.
